# Logo Creation System Prompt

Use this prompt in Nano Banana for generating the application logo.

---

## Prompt

Design a minimal, modern app logo for "Snacks" — an office snack ordering application.

**Concept:**
- The logo should represent food/snacks ordering in a workplace setting
- Combine a snack element (like a bowl of snacks, popcorn, or a cookie) with a subtle ordering/selection cue (like a checkmark, tap gesture, or a simple card)
- Keep it friendly, warm, and approachable — not corporate or cold

**Style Guidelines:**
- Notion-inspired aesthetic: soft, warm, hand-drawn feel with clean geometry
- Use a soft, warm color palette: peach (#FDECC8), soft orange (#FADEC9), warm brown (#937264), with an off-white or cream background
- Rounded shapes, no sharp edges
- Flat design with minimal detail — no gradients, no 3D, no shadows
- Should work clearly at small sizes (app icon on phone home screen)
- Single icon mark, no text in the logo

**Shape & Composition:**
- Rounded square or circle container (standard app icon shape)
- Centered, symmetrical composition
- One primary element (the snack) with one small accent (checkmark, sparkle, or dot)
- Generous whitespace — don't overcrowd

**What to avoid:**
- Realistic food photography style
- Too many colors (max 3-4)
- Text or wordmarks inside the icon
- Complex illustrations with many elements
- Generic restaurant/fork-and-knife symbols
- Overly playful or cartoonish style

**Output:**
- Square format (1024x1024)
- Clean transparent or solid cream background
- Suitable for both iOS and Android app icons
